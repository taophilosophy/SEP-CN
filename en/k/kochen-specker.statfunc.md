#### Supplement to The Kochen-Specker Theorem

## STAT FUNC from FUNC

Indeed, STAT FUNC follows immediately from FUNC. Given *A*, *f*, and |φ> as defined in FUNC, we have:

> *v*(*f*(*A*))^{|φ>} = *f*(*v*(*A*))^{|φ>},

where *f*(*A*) is a new observable. Now, by the statistical algorithm:

> **STAT FUNC:** Given *A*, *f*, and φ as defined in FUNC, then, for an arbitrary real number *b*:
>
> > prob[*v*(*f*(*A*))^{|φ>} = *b*] = prob[*f*(*v*(*A*))^{|φ>} = *b*]
