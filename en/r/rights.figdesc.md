## Long descriptions for the figure in Rights

### Figure description

We are describing the diagram using a table. Anything in the second-order column of the table can match with anything in the first-order column. The figure has arrows drawn from both second-order items (Power and Privilege) to one of the first-order items (Claim).

|   | **second-order**: rights over the first-order rights | **first-order**: rights over the computer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You have | **Power** to waive, annul, or transfer your | **Privilege** to use the computer |
| **Immunity** against others altering your | **Claim** against others using the computer |   |
