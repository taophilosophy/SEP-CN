## Long descriptions for some figures in Bayesian Epistemology

### Figure 1 description

A horizontal line with three equidistant dots on it. Under the leftmost dot is a bracket with the words “*H* is true”. Under the two leftmost dots (and under the first bracket) is another bracket with the words “*E* is true”.

### Figure 2 description

Two bar charts with an arrow labeled “Conditionalization on *E*” pointing from the left one to the right one.

The left bar chart consists of three equidistant vertical bars over dots labeled 1/2, 1/4, and 1/4. The bar heights match the label descriptions. Under the leftmost bar dot is a bracket with the words “*H* is true”. Under the two leftmost bar dots (and under the first bracket) is another bracket with the words “*E* is true”.

The right bar chart consists of three equidistant vertical bars over dots labeled 2/3, 1/3, and 0. The bar heights match the label descriptions. Under the leftmost bar dot is a bracket with the words “*H* is true”. Under the two leftmost bar dots (and under the first bracket) is another bracket with the words “*E* is true”.

### Figure 3 description

This is similar to Figure 2 except for the bar heights. There are no labels on the bars, but, we'll give approximate heights.

Two bar charts with an arrow labeled “Conditionalization on *E*” pointing from the left one to the right one.

The left bar chart consists of three equidistant vertical bars over dots. The bar heights are approximately 1/8, 1/16, and 13/16. Under the leftmost bar dot is a bracket with the words “*H* is true”. Under the two leftmost bar dots (and under the first bracket) is another bracket with the words “*E* is true”.

The right bar chart consists of three equidistant vertical bars over dots. The bar heights are approximately 2/3, 1/3, and 0. Under the leftmost bar dot is a bracket with the words “*H* is true”. Under the two leftmost bar dots (and under the first bracket) is another bracket with the words “*E* is true”.
